### Grew Marketing-Driven Sales by 38% While Cutting Ad Spend in Half

**Industry:** eCommerce
**Tools:** Amazon Ads, Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Power BI, SQL

#### The Problem
A fast-growing eCommerce business was spending heavily on advertising but seeing diminishing returns. Campaigns were running on gut instinct — there was no reliable data to identify what was working, what wasn't, and where money was being wasted. The Advertising Cost of Sale (ACOS) stood at 18%, eating deep into margins.

#### What Was Done
A full data and analytics infrastructure was built from the ground up. An automated Amazon Ads optimization dashboard was created to surface real-time performance data, enabling faster and smarter campaign decisions. Category-wise sales analysis improved targeting accuracy by 15%. Google Search Console and Analytics were implemented to fix SEO gaps and improve organic visibility. Dynamic dashboards in Power BI and SQL gave the team live KPI tracking, replacing manual reporting with always-on visibility.

#### The Result
- **38% growth** in marketing-driven sales
- **ACOS dropped from 18% to 9%** — ad spend efficiency doubled
- **15% improvement** in campaign targeting accuracy
- **50%+ overall business sales growth** during the same period

> Every rupee spent on ads started working twice as hard — not by spending more, but by knowing exactly where to spend it.

### Saved $5,500 Every Month by Eliminating Fabric Wastage

**Industry:** Textile Manufacturing
**Tools:** KPI Tracking, Process Automation, Reporting Systems

#### The Problem
A garment manufacturing business was losing thousands of dollars each month to fabric wastage. There was no systematic way to track consumption across production units, identify root causes, or hold departments accountable. Waste was being written off as an unavoidable cost of doing business.

#### What Was Done
A full process audit was conducted across the production floor, mapping how fabric moved from procurement through to finished goods. KPI tracking was introduced at the unit level, fabric and accessories requirement calculations were automated for each product — removing human error from the most costly stage — and real-time reporting was built to give management visibility they had never had before.

#### The Result
- **$5,500 saved every month** — recurring, compounding impact
- **3% reduction** in fabric wastage across production units
- Full visibility into a process that had previously been a black box

> What was once written off as unavoidable became a controlled, measurable, and solved problem.

### Eliminated 13+ Hours of Manual Work Per Week Across Reporting and Data Entry

**Industry:** Services / ERP Operations
**Tools:** Python, Power BI, ERP Automation, Document Processing

#### The Problem
The operations team was spending 13+ hours every week on work that should never have been manual. Management reports took 6–7 hours to compile each week. Two separate weekly reports consumed another 2 and 5 hours respectively. On top of that, material receipts from scanned documents were being entered into the ERP by hand, and invoices and journal entries were being processed manually from PDFs — skilled time wasted on repetitive, error-prone tasks.

#### What Was Done
Each process was systematically automated. Management reporting was converted into fully automated live dashboards — no more weekly compilation. The two manual weekly reports were replaced with automated data pipelines. A document automation system was built so that scanned files dropped into a folder were automatically processed into Material Receipt entries in the ERP. The same logic was applied to invoice and journal entry generation from PDFs. Bank reconciliation was also automated, removing a persistent manual bottleneck.

#### The Result
- **13+ hours of manual work eliminated per week**
- Reporting became real-time instead of retrospective
- Data entry errors reduced to near-zero
- Team freed to focus on analysis and decision-making — not data wrangling

> The team didn't get faster at manual work. The manual work stopped existing.

### Replaced Operational Chaos with a Unified Planning Dashboard

**Industry:** Services / Distribution
**Tools:** Custom Dashboard, ERP Integration, Python

#### The Problem
The business had no planning or visibility module in their legacy ERP. Dispatch, purchase, and inventory planning were managed entirely through phone calls and emails between Sales and Operations. This created a cascade of problems that compounded daily:

- **Zero visibility** — no one had a clear picture of what was happening across the business
- **Highly error-prone** — decisions made on incomplete or outdated information
- **Dissatisfied customers** — incorrect communications, missed commitments, and last-minute surprises
- **Planning chaos** — changes at the last moment disrupted production, procurement, and delivery
- **Underutilized resources** — without data, people and assets were routinely misallocated
- **Unoptimized operations** — inefficiencies were invisible and therefore unaddressed

Every department was operating in its own silo, held together by informal communication that couldn't scale.

#### What Was Done
A custom Dispatch, Purchase and Inventory Planning tool was designed and built, integrated directly with the existing ERP. Sales and Operations now collaborated on a single live dashboard — with real-time data replacing all back-and-forth communication. Automated material request workflows were also introduced, capturing operational data that had never existed before.

#### The Result
- **Single source of truth** across Sales, Operations, and Dispatch
- Manual coordination calls and emails largely eliminated
- Customer communications became accurate and timely
- Last-minute planning changes reduced dramatically
- Resource allocation became data-driven and optimized
- **1.5 hours saved per day** across branches on material requests alone
- Business gained visibility into operational data it had never had before

> The business didn't just save time. It gained control — for the first time.

### Automated Real-Time Order Notifications via WhatsApp for a B2B Business

**Industry:** B2B Distribution / Services
**Tools:** WhatsApp Business API, ERP Integration, Automation Workflows

#### The Problem
In a B2B business where orders are created by the company on behalf of customers, customers had no real-time visibility into their orders. There was no automated communication — customers either had to call in to check status or wait to be manually informed, creating unnecessary back-and-forth and eroding trust.

#### What Was Done
A WhatsApp automation was implemented that triggered an instant, personalized notification to the customer every time an order was created on their behalf. The workflow was integrated directly with the order management system so no manual action was required.

#### The Result
- **Customers informed instantly** the moment an order is created
- Inbound "where's my order?" queries eliminated
- Professional, consistent customer communication at zero marginal effort
- Stronger customer trust and satisfaction

> A simple automation turned order creation into a customer experience moment.
